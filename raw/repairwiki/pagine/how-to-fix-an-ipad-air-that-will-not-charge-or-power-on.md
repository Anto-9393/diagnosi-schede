# How to fix an iPad Air that will not charge or power on

> Fonte: [How to fix an iPad Air that will not charge or power on — repair.wiki](https://repair.wiki/w/How_to_fix_an_iPad_Air_that_will_not_charge_or_power_on), CC BY-SA 3.0. Autori: vedi cronologia.

## Problem description
Repairing an issue where the battery will not charge and the iPad does not boot

## Symptoms
- Device shows no signs of life.
- When connected to a charger the iPad pulls zero amps.

## Solution
Most commonly caused by a bad tristar chip.
